# SM2 Enhanced

A free, fan-made launcher and mod for **Spider-Man 2 (2004, GameCube)**, played on the Dolphin emulator.

## ⬇️ Download

**[Get the latest version](https://github.com/JohnSwingMods/sm2-enhanced/releases/latest)** → download `SM2 Enhanced.exe` and open it.

Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box: click **More info** and then **Run anyway** (the app isn't signed yet).

You need your **own copy of the game**. No game files are included.

## What it does

- **One-click setup:** installs Dolphin, finds your disc, sets everything up
- **HD textures:** downloads and installs the community HD packs from ModDB for you
- **40+ suits** with a 3D preview, plus an alternate suit you switch to by tapping RB
- **60 FPS, no motion blur, HUD fixed for 16:9**, graphics presets up to 4K
- **Gameplay codes:** dive with L3, wall runs and web zips that keep your speed, landing momentum
- **All 5 versions:** USA, UK, Italy, Germany, France (European versions patched to 60 Hz)
- **100% save included**, any controller (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro), save states with photos
- **Export** a ready-to-play mod for Android, Xbox and Mac
